## Important limitations
- A passing suite establishes test execution, not independent diagnostic correctness
- A literal assertion link is not proof of complete fault coverage
- Negative/exclusion assertions are not positive fault diagnosis validation
- Assertions with computed IDs, multi-line syntax or fixtures may be missed
- Equipment scope is declared in rules; not proven by test execution
- Other suites and scenario datasets remain unreviewed
- No manufacturer or textbook reference validation performed
