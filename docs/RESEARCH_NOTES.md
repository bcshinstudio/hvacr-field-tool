# WIC Knowledge Research Notes --- v0.2

Primary manufacturer sources used in this expansion: - Danfoss cold-room
troubleshooting: fault families, charge, TXV/EEV, icing, airflow, load,
controls and defrost. - Danfoss Ref Tools: low suction and high
condensing/head-pressure cause lists. - Copeland Refrigeration Manual
Part 5: restrictions, drier temperature decrease, abnormal discharge
pressure causes. - Copeland system-design/training material: sight-glass
limitations, TEV application, compression-ratio/discharge-temperature
and floodback relationships. - Parker Sporlan literature: TEV and
filter-drier technical framework. - Heatcraft preventive
maintenance/FAQs: fan/coil checks, leak evidence, superheat by
application, drier/moisture checks, wet-system/TXV icing example.

Important curation decision: This library records manufacturer-supported
relationships without turning typical symptoms into universal one-to-one
diagnoses. High head, low suction, bubbles, icing, and high superheat
are treated as evidence requiring context and discrimination.
